Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Welcome to SweatCount. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SweatCount app.

Information We Collect

  • Name: Used to personalize your profile and experience in the app.
  • Email Address: Used for authentication, login, and account recovery purposes.
  • Progress Data: Includes your workout days, streaks, and habit tracking to show your progress and achievements.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve app functionality.
  • To sync your progress and maintain your streak data.
  • To communicate important updates or support responses.

Data Storage

All user data is securely stored using Supabase cloud infrastructure. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data with any third parties.

Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data against unauthorized access or misuse.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
