Privacy Policy — SweatCount

Last Updated: December 2025

SweatCount (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a fitness habit tracking app designed to help users build consistency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SweatCount mobile app.

Information We Collect

  • Name: Used to display your profile name.
  • Email Address: Used for account creation, login, password recovery, and communication.
  • Progress & Activity Data: Includes streaks, completed days, gym reminders, meditation timers and step tracking data (if enabled on your device).

Permissions

The app may request:

  • Notification access — to send workout reminders.
  • Activity/step data — used only to show your statistics inside the app.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Data Storage

Your account and progress data are securely stored using Supabase cloud services.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to any third party.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your fitness progress
  • To send optional reminders/notifications
  • To improve app quality and support

Data Retention

Your data is stored as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

SweatCount is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

You may request:

  • Account deletion
  • Data review
  • Update or correction

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when needed. Updates will be posted here.
